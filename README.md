## Troubleshooting Methodology

When investigating a technical issue, I generally follow a structured approach:

1. Understand the customer's problem and expected behaviour.
2. Establish the scope and impact of the issue.
3. Attempt to reproduce the problem where possible.
4. Break the issue down into individual technical components.
5. Gather evidence from logs, configuration and diagnostic tools.
6. Form a hypothesis based on the available evidence.
7. Test the hypothesis with controlled changes.
8. Identify the root cause.
9. Implement or recommend a resolution.
10. Document the solution and confirm the issue is resolved.
